Good roleplay prompts do one job: they drop two characters into a situation that already has tension. You should be able to read a prompt and immediately picture the first line. Below are 60 original prompts grouped by genre, plus tips for turning any of them into a full scene. Use them with a partner, with an AI character, or as inspiration for a character you want to build.

## How to use these prompts

Each prompt gives you a setup and a hook. You and your partner decide who plays which role, then start in the middle of the action. A few tips:

- **Start late.** Skip the arrival and introductions. Begin when something is already happening.
- **Add one personal stake.** Give your character a private reason to care.
- **Let the AI director fill in the world.** On SweetScene, the director sets the scene and voices side characters, so you can focus on your role.

If you prefer something ready to go, the [scenarios page](/scenarios) has polished setups like a midnight train, a masquerade, office rivals, and a space station lockdown.

## Turning a prompt into a great scene

A prompt is a spark. Here is how to make it a story.

### Give each character a secret

In prompt 34, maybe the detective is not a real detective, and the suspect knows it. Secrets create layers that come out over time.

### Find the first line

Write an opening that shows action and invites a reply. For prompt 2:

> *The bell over the bakery door jingles. You walk in holding a single blue iris, the sixth this week, and set it on the counter.* "Okay. I need to know who keeps sending these, and why they always come here."

### Escalate

Every few exchanges, add a complication. Someone arrives, a clue appears, the weather turns. In a multiplayer scene, the AI director is great at this, and you can prompt it by having your character open a door or ask a side character a question.

### Aim for an ending

Good scenes end on a strong beat: a reveal, a choice, a confession, or a cliffhanger. On SweetScene, that is the moment both players decide whether to reveal profiles and keep playing in DMs, or skip to the next match.

## FAQ

### What makes a good roleplay prompt?

A good prompt puts two characters in a situation with built-in tension and a clear hook, so you can picture the first line right away.

### Can I use these prompts with AI characters?

Yes. They work for multiplayer scenes with an AI director, for solo chats with AI characters, and as seeds for characters you create yourself.

### How do I start a roleplay from a prompt?

Start in the middle of the action, give your character a personal stake, and write an opening that shows what they are doing and invites your partner to respond.

### Are these prompts safe-for-work?

Yes. Every prompt here is safe-for-work, including the romance and dark sections, which focus on emotion, mystery, and drama.
